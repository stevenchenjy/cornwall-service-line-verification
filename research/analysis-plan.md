# Analysis plan — baseline and workflow before allocation

1. Preserve the complete locality snapshot and before/after metadata/count receipts with SHA256. Pin the combined snapshot; dataset-level update time is not line freshness.
2. Audit raw categories, material states, recorded methods and repeated keys without deleting rows. Add category disagreement checks within repeated groups and preserve review queues locally.
3. Keep public material `current_public_side_sl` distinct from public verification method `public_sl_material`. The attached official dictionary and guidance now support that distinction; conflicting API descriptions remain preserved. Use the [documented mapping](data-dictionary.md) and source receipts without treating documentary support as departmental confirmation. Preserve historical public lead status and segment/ownership facts for category review.
4. Establish a dated system-specific allocation baseline with stable pseudonymous IDs, source-row mapping, side materials/evidence and accepted overall category. Include relevant utility lines outside the locality. If unavailable, keep the public raw-record audit as a narrower endpoint.
5. Use the [workflow specification](workflow-specification.md) to separate reported labels, reviewed evidence and accepted final decisions. Support records/resident/field sequences and changing eligibility, failures and retries. Do not treat the frozen 57-row resident-only bound as the role of resident assistance in combined workflows.
6. Bring review forward: examine the first baseline audit and transition specification before an optimizer, strategy result or map. Record the actual reviewer and evidence inspected; inline self-review is not independent review.
7. Request a recent anonymized linked attempt log, including unsuccessful attempts, effort components, evidence acceptance and final decision. If only aggregates exist, request denominators and effort by initial state/action/sequence, including joint outcomes and repeats. No arbitrary fixed pilot sample size is specified.
8. Obtain current practice, acceptance rules, binding resource budget and coverage priorities. Keep costs/rates null until calibrated or explicitly varied as hypothetical scenarios. Stock method counts cannot estimate success probabilities.
9. Compare a small set of department-approved sequences against current practice under equal resources. Retain dependence and selection limitations, uncertainty, hard cases, retries and remaining gaps. Recommend no local optimum until the evidence gates pass.
10. Review relevant work and local contractor/department analyses. A public map is optional and requires reviewed aggregation. No contact, fieldwork or outreach is part of this phase.

Second-review amendment (2026-10-20): final-decision acceptance must use pinned and attributed source/rule records with matching state and sufficient category-specific evidence. Record rejected/deferred review effort, separate resident assessment, and preserve superseding correction history. Follow research/data-intake-plan.md; examine initial difficulty, action history and selection before comparing observed rates.
