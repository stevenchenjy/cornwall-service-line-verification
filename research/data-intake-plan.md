## Minimum intake and acceptance

1. Preserve received exports, source documents and permissions unchanged in restricted/ignored storage with timestamp and SHA256. Obtain a dated stable-ID system-specific population, including relevant other localities. Keep the source-row crosswalk local; do not infer identity from repeated or differently spelled addresses.
2. Confirm the material/method mapping and applicable category-specific rules with the department/state. A curator records the source, reviewer identifier, date and exact requirements. Hashes establish integrity; they do not authenticate a department or demonstrate legal sufficiency.
3. A final accepted decision needs a pinned structured decision record, its original source, reviewed rule record/source, matching case/system/baseline/evidence fingerprint and the side evidence required by that category rule. Missing or unvalidated evidence remains pending. Real documents must be substantively checked by an authorized reviewer before setting validation status. No actual local policy is encoded by synthetic examples.
4. Record classification acceptance, rejection and deferral as separate review events with effort. Keep resident submission distinct from departmental evidence assessment when they are separate activities. An unsupported acceptance becomes a deferred review with the validation error retained. Corrections require a new documented decision, the superseded ID, reason, replacement evidence and immutable before/after history.
5. Map actual source exports to the attempt schema without zero-filling unavailable effort. Confirm whether unsuccessful attempts and repeats are captured; if a denominator is incomplete, do not estimate an action success probability from it. Unknown effort makes time-efficiency estimation unavailable for the affected comparison.

## Selection and comparison design

Retain initial material/evidence state, prior failures/actions, action order, available property/access characteristics, selection reason, date and any existing coarse travel grouping. Ask how assignments were chosen. Distinguish differences in case difficulty from method performance. Compare within supported initial-state/history groups and report uncertainty and unobserved selection limits; do not call raw success differences causal effects.

Before comparing sequences, agree on the actual current workflow, binding budget and coverage priorities. Require adequate overlap between action groups; when historical assignment is strongly selective or unsuccessful work is missing, identify the missing measurement or design a separately authorized prospective pilot. Do not prescribe a fixed sample size without an outcome, uncertainty target and feasible design.

## Advance gate

Strategy evaluation requires a confirmed unique-line baseline, accepted field/rule semantics, actual current comparator, linked attempts including failures/reviews/repeats, defensible effort/outcomes and resource/coverage priorities. If only public records or incomplete aggregates exist, retain the narrower audit/design endpoint and document which comparisons remain unidentified. A map remains optional.
