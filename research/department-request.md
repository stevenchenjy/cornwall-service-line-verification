Could we first confirm what existing records and analyses are available?

1. The agreed system/service-area boundary and a current dated inventory using stable pseudonymous line IDs, with both material labels, evidence/method and acceptance status, and the accepted overall category. A defined recent period of pseudonymous linked attempt histories, including successes, failures, nonresponse, initial case characteristics/state, prior attempts, selection reason, preparation/travel/contact-field/administration-review effort, final decisions and follow-up. Preserve rejected/deferred review effort, same-day action order and decision-correction history. Please identify incomplete event capture, whether time is elapsed or staff person-time, and treatment of shared work; leave unavailable effort missing.
5. The staff-time or cost constraint and coverage priorities that should define a fair comparison with current practice.

If only aggregates are available, attempted/accepted/failed/repeated counts and total effort by initial state and action sequence, including joint outcomes, would help establish feasibility. Please confirm whether this is the intended study system and how a current dated inventory relates to that summary and the separate public locality extract. We have not treated those sources as the same population or reporting date.
