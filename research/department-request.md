# Service-line baseline and workflow request - ready for review, not sent

Suggested subject: Cornwall service-line inventory and verification workflow - minimum research inputs

Dear Water Department,

I am preparing an independent, AI-assisted study of how records review, resident assistance and field work can be combined to obtain accepted service-line classifications under limited staff time. The public locality inventory is useful for identifying information gaps, but it cannot establish current unique-line counts or strategy effectiveness.

Could we first confirm what existing records and analyses are available?

1. The agreed system/service-area boundary and a current dated inventory using stable pseudonymous line IDs, with both material labels, evidence/method and acceptance status, and the accepted overall category. Relevant system lines outside the Cornwall-on-Hudson locality filter should be included. Please retain a restricted crosswalk to public rows and explain repeated or differently spelled address records.
2. The authoritative state/department category-specific acceptance rules and field mapping, including how submissions, inconclusive evidence, rejected/deferred reviews and corrections to accepted decisions are handled.
3. The actual current sequence of contact, records work, resident submissions, departmental evidence/classification review, field escalation and repeat attempts. Any existing contractor/workflow analysis and the intended departmental decision would help define the comparison.
4. A defined recent period of pseudonymous linked attempt histories, including successes, failures, nonresponse, initial case characteristics/state, prior attempts, selection reason, preparation/travel/contact-field/administration-review effort, final decisions and follow-up. Preserve rejected/deferred review effort and decision-correction history.
5. The staff-time or cost constraint and coverage priorities that should define a fair comparison with current practice.

The attached schema is a proposed intake aid, not a demand that the department create new records or adopt our software format. An existing export can be mapped and documented. If only aggregates are available, attempted/accepted/failed/repeated counts and total effort by initial state and action sequence, including joint outcomes, would help establish feasibility. No household names, precise addresses or coordinates are requested in the analysis export.

Thank you for considering this request.
