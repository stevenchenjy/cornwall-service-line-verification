# Service-line verification technical request - ready for user review, not sent

Suggested subject: Existing service-line inventory definitions and verification records

This technical attachment is prepared for the coordinated Cornwall research introduction. It concerns service-line classification only; the water-accounting request has separate records and permissions.

Our question is whether department-approved sequences of records review, resident assistance and field work could produce more accepted complete-line classifications under the same staff-time constraint as current practice. The public inventory cannot establish current unique-line counts or local effectiveness.

## First response: existing records and definitions

Please share existing documents, links or native-format summaries where available. A short statement that a record is unavailable is useful; please do not create a retrospective log or estimate missing time for this study.

1. **System and inventory scope.** Confirm the intended PWS and service-area boundary, including relevant lines outside the Cornwall-on-Hudson locality. Share the latest dated inventory or existing category summary with its cut-off, inclusion rules, field definitions and explanation of repeated address records. If a line-level export is readily available, use stable pseudonymous line IDs and preserve source material/method/category labels separately from evidence and classification acceptance. An aggregate summary is an acceptable first response, but does not establish line identity.
2. **Dates, rules and history.** Explain how that inventory relates to the public locality extract (1,110 raw rows / 808 Unknown) and the separate NY3503526 summary (2,553 total / 1,514 Unknown; displayed certification `10/15/24`). We infer no common population or trend. Provide the applicable classification/verification guidance, version and effective dates, any system-specific approvals, historical upstream-lead requirements and segment limits versus ownership. The archived August 2024 dictionary and August 2025 guidance do not establish your current rules.
3. **Actual workflow and recording coverage.** Share an existing procedure, form, contractor analysis or brief description covering records work, contact failures, resident submissions, evidence assessment, accepted/rejected/deferred decisions, retries, escalation and corrections. Identify which activities, outcomes, dates, links and time measures are actually recorded, in what system and over what usable period. Please distinguish a filed category from a reviewed accepted decision.
4. **Decision and capacity.** State the practical question the department would want this study to inform, current staffing/time or cost limits, and coverage priorities or access constraints. An existing workload summary or stated planning constraint is sufficient initially; unmeasured capacity may remain unknown.

## Conditional follow-up: only records already held in usable form

After scope and availability are understood, a minimum empirical package would link a dated stable-ID inventory to an agreed recent period of actions and decisions. Existing separate inventory, work-order, contact, review or contractor exports can be mapped by the researchers; the department need not adopt our schema.

Where held, retain action order and initial states/history, unsuccessful and repeated work, submitted versus accepted evidence, joint public/customer outcomes, dated final decisions and corrections, selection reasons and preparation/travel/contact-field/administration-review effort. Identify elapsed time versus staff person-time, shared work and missing capture. Unavailable fields stay missing. Accepted corrections need a documented replacement decision, superseded ID and reason; failed corrections retain available metadata and effort even when those fields were never recorded.

Smaller alternatives are welcome: an existing inventory-only export plus rule documents; separate native logs with existing stable links; or already-held aggregate tables by initial state, action/sequence and period with explicit attempted/failed/repeated counts, distinct accepted cases, joint outcomes and effort where measured. Please describe denominators, exclusions and suppression. Unlinked success totals, marginal side counts or inventory method frequencies alone cannot support sequence effectiveness or staff-time comparisons. We will document these limits rather than request invented history.

No names, addresses, precise coordinates, contact details or unrestricted source decisions are requested in the analysis export. Any identity crosswalk should remain with the department unless separately agreed through a secure restricted route; it must never be included in the public packet. Received source records, permissions and validation evidence remain restricted. Only reviewed aggregate outputs would be considered for publication, with disclosure review for small cells.

The [intake packet](intake-handoff.md) provides optional headers, a 12-case human/departmental review guide and the analysis limits for each available evidence level. We would first confirm what can be supplied before asking for extensive exports or staff review.
